How do I book an appointment?

You can book online, by phone, or via Instagram DM. Walk-ins are limited, so we recommend booking ahead.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We operate mainly by appointment, but feel free to message us – we’ll try to fit you in if there’s a gap.

What kind of products do you use?

We use high-quality, non-toxic, and cruelty-free products that are safe and nourishing for natural nails.

What’s the service duration?

Most services take 45–90 minutes depending on the treatment and any add-ons like art or extensions.

Can I bring my own nail design?

Yes! We love custom inspiration. Bring photos or ideas – we’ll make it work with your nail shape and style.

Is gel safe for my nails?

Yes – when applied and removed properly. We’re careful to protect your nails and keep them healthy underneath.
